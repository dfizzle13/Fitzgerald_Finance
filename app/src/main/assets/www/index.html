How slush coverage works

Recorded net income after outflows remains unassigned. Spending that crosses its weekly, monthly, or yearly line-item limit is highlighted and counted as coverage.
Unbudgeted purchases are fully flagged until assigned to a budget line. If recorded outflows exceed income, the net slush balance can become negative.
